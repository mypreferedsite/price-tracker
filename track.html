What this page measures

Each analysis page shows a verdict (BUY, HOLD, SELL, WAIT or NEUTRAL). Here the same verdict is rebuilt for every past hour from your saved prices, using only what was known at that hour (no peeking at the future). Then we look at what the price did 24 hours, 72 hours and 168 hours (one week) later.

The default settings of each page are used. The rules were reasoned in advance, not adjusted to fit this history. Even so, treat the result as a first impression, not proof: the history is short, the hours overlap (a verdict that lasts 20 hours is counted 20 times, which makes results look more reliable than they are), and assets measured against the same dollar often move together.

Edge = the average return after this verdict minus the average return after any hour. A BUY verdict is useful if its edge is positive, a SELL verdict if its edge is negative.
